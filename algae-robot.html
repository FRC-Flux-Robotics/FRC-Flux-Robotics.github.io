Algae Robot Configuration

FRC Team 10413 FLUX Robotics - 2025 Season - Intake Mechanism Robot

Overview

The Algae robot is configured for the Intake mechanism, designed to collect and process Algae game pieces. It uses a swerve drivetrain with a pneumatic intake system and roller mechanism for game piece manipulation.
